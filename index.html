Utmatic was built for content teams that create a high volume of product-focused documents. If you're working on catalogs, brochures, spec sheets, or any materials filled with SKUs or part numbers, manually adding hyperlinks is slow and repetitive. Utmatic automates that process with a simple, streamlined workflow—no design tools or manual effort required.

A faster way to add links and tracking

Utmatic makes it easy to instantly hyperlink and append UTM parameters to every product number and SKU in your documents. The process is simple, repeatable, and fast—no manual work, no wasted time.

  1. 1. Upload your document
    Start by selecting your file. PDF processing is available to all users, with InDesign support included in upgraded plans.
  2. 2. Define your link rules
    Tell Utmatic how to identify product numbers in your document and where each link should point. You can also build and save custom presets for repeated use, making future workflows even faster.
  3. 3. Submit and get your finished file
    Utmatic scans your file, finds every matching item, and links it automatically based on your rules. In seconds, you’ll have a fully linked, ready-to-use file—no manual work required.
