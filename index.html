    How to Map Document Areas

    • Click and drag to draw a rectangle over the PDF area you want to map.
    • Optionally, enter a name for the mapping area in the input above the selected area.
    • Set the page range and whether it applies to all, even, or odd pages.
    • Click Apply to add the mapping area to your list.
    • To remove a mapping, click × next to it in the list.
    Tip: You can adjust range/options before applying.
